Where you start

You don't need to code.
You need an idea.

Maybe you want to launch a service, organise a project or make an idea more concrete. In Iniciante you set up your workspace, pick a first result you can actually build and learn to direct agents to build it.

Bring a computer, an internet connection and the confidence to use email, open web pages and save files. Plan for about eight hours of practice between sessions.

If your idea is a full app, we start with a small part you can build and test.

Iniciante / The course

You learn while
you build your project.

Six two-hour sessions with short breaks. Every session moves your project forward.

Planned start on Sunday, 3 January 2027, once at least six people sign up. Vote for the time that suits you: 10am, 4pm or 8pm, Lisbon time.

01Set up the base

Set up email, accounts and access. Get to know ChatGPT, Claude, Codex and Cursor, and save your first file in your main workspace.

02Give the project direction

Define the goal, the first version, priorities, limits and what counts as done. Turn an idea into a short plan you can carry out.

03Organise the work

Design a small folder structure, keep reference versions and write instructions so any conversation can pick the project back up.

04Delegate and build

Give an agent the context, the task, the limits and the expected result. Follow the changes and build a first usable version.

05Coordinate and review

Pass work between a builder and a reviewer, weigh their differences and decide what to fix. Test the result and avoid endless rounds of review.

06Build a repeatable workflow

Make a change on your own and repeat the cycle: goal, task, build, review, test and decision. Leave clear next steps.

Before you start

A few important
answers.

When does it start and how much is it?

The planned start is Sunday, 3 January 2027, once at least six people sign up. Six Sundays, two hours each. In the form you can vote for 10am, 4pm or 8pm, Lisbon time. The price is €240 per participant. You get the confirmed time and the class conditions before any commitment or payment.

I don't live in Portugal. Can I join?

Yes. The course is online and in English. Sessions run on Lisbon time, which is GMT in January: 8pm in Lisbon is 3pm in New York and Toronto, 5pm in São Paulo and 9pm in Lagos.

Do I need paid tools?

You get to know ChatGPT, Claude, Codex and Cursor and practise in one main workspace. You don't need to master four tools or pay for four subscriptions.

Do the agents talk to each other automatically?

You learn to pass context between a builder and a reviewer and to oversee decisions and results. That hand-off can be done by hand. Automatic coordination can be shown where it's available; we don't promise a universal automatic link between the four tools.

Do I need to have a business?

No. You can bring an idea for a service, a personal project or something else. We pick a first version that fits the Iniciante course.

Does the course guarantee sales or a certificate?

No. It doesn't promise income, a job or a professional certificate. The aim is for you to organise your project, direct agents, build and test a first version and carry on by yourself.

What happens after I send the form?

Your request and your preferred time reach Galzu. Once six people have signed up and we've checked which times work, we'll contact you to confirm the conditions for this class. Pre-registration doesn't sign you up to a newsletter or confirm a place.
